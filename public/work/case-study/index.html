Inside The Project
Brand Story

We brought Savanna Café's identity to life — a complete brand,
packaging, and print system built around warmth, craft, and coffee.

We aimed to bring Savanna Café’s vision to life — an identity that feels as warm and crafted as the coffee they serve.

This project reinforced the power of a cohesive identity: from logo and color palette to packaging and menu design, every element works together. The café’s new look was met with glowing feedback, and customers now recognise Savanna at a glance.

Brand & visuals

One challenge was balancing a modern look with an earthy, artisanal feel. Our team developed a flexible brand system — organic textures, warm tones, and clean typography — that works across print and digital without losing its soul.

Designing the packaging meant considering shelf appeal and sustainability. We created cup designs, labels, and store signage that invite customers in and tell the story of the beans inside.

Creative research

We studied the local coffee scene, reviewed competitor branding, and listened to what customers love about Savanna to define the direction that matters most.

This research phase helped align the café’s personality with customer expectations, giving the design and production team a clear direction before execution.

Project result

The final identity delivered a warmer, more memorable brand — one that stands out on the shelf and in photos shared across social media.

By combining a clear logo, consistent color system, and print-ready packaging, Savanna now supports stronger recognition and sustainable growth.
